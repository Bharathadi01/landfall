# Landfall — Humanitarian Impact & Scientific Evidence Grounding

## 1. Authoritative Scientific Research Grounding

Landfall's core premise—providing cash *before* disaster landfall—is grounded in rigorous peer-reviewed empirical research:

### A. World Food Programme (WFP) Randomized Controlled Trial (2020)
*Citation:* WFP Anticipatory Action Impact Evaluation: Bangladesh Monsoon Floods (2020).  
*Key Findings:*
1. **Predatory Debt Avoidance:** Households receiving unconditional digital cash 3 days before peak flood crest borrowed 40% less at predatory local interest rates than control households.
2. **Asset Protection:** Pre-disaster recipients were twice as likely to evacuate essential livestock, medicine, and critical household property.
3. **Caloric Security:** Post-disaster hunger scores were significantly lower, as families purchased non-perishable food and potable water while local markets were still operating.

### B. World Bank Research on Anticipatory Liquidity (2024)
*Citation:* Advance Research on the Timing Value of Pre-Disaster Cash Transfers.  
*Key Findings:*
1. **The Timing Multiplier:** Every $1.00 delivered in anticipatory humanitarian cash yields up to $7.00 in avoided losses, displacement expenses, and emergency shelter costs.
2. **The 100-Day Delay:** Traditional international and federal aid pipelines require an average of 100 days from disaster onset to cash receipt. By shifting disbursement into the 48-to-72 hour pre-landfall warning window, community relief eliminates the costliest phase of personal displacement.

## 3. Mathematical Capital Efficiency & PayPal Rail Analysis

### Domestic Payout Efficiency
Traditional relief organizations report overheads between 20% and 30%. Landfall leverages PayPal Payouts domestic flat fee pricing:

$$\text{Rail Fee Overhead} = \frac{\$0.25}{\$100.00} = 0.25\%$$

$$\text{Net Direct Relief Delivered} = 100\% - 0.25\% = 99.75\%$$

For a standard $100.00 pre-storm grant:
- **Net Aid Received:** $100.00 directly to the recipient's PayPal account.
- **Transaction Overhead:** $0.25 paid by the community fund.
- **Direct Relief Efficiency:** **99.75%** of gross funds reach the family.

## 4. Community Accountability & Appeals System

- **Transparent Eligibility Rules:** Rules are published in plain language (`en`, `es`, `ht`, `vi`, `zh`) with deterministic scoring.
- **Appeals & Invalidation Invariant:** If an appeal is upheld and alters a household's priority tier, any pending unsubmitted payout plan referencing the household is invalidated automatically and requires fresh dual passkey authorization.
- **Alternative Assistance Routing:** For unbanked residents without smartphones or PayPal accounts, the platform tracks offline community vouchers and volunteer-assisted cash cards.
