# Stakeholder validation kit

**Status: no validation is claimed.** The public count on `/validate` is read from the database; it is 0 until real people
answer. Nothing in this repository quotes, names or implies an endorsement that did not happen.

## What to send (30 seconds)
> Hi — I built a prototype (PayPal Sandbox, fake money) for relief funds: it tracks each payment past PayPal's "SUCCESS"
> to the household's own answer, and an AI helps a person investigate stuck payments. Does that problem exist for you?
> Two minutes, blunt answers welcome: https://landfall-web-t5rw.onrender.com/validate

## Who to ask (people who actually run or audit disaster-relief payments)
- Community-foundation rapid-response funds (e.g. a Community Foundation's disaster fund) — fund managers and grants staff
- Local mutual-aid funds and neighbourhood relief groups
- County / city emergency-management and long-term-recovery group staff
- Florida / Gulf-coast VOAD and COAD members; United Way 211 disaster teams
- Cash-assistance programme staff at relief nonprofits (they know the "did the money reach them?" problem first-hand)
- Residents who have received disaster aid (for the resident-side flow: is the claim step clear? the languages?)

## Interview guide (15 minutes)
1. Walk me through the last time you sent money to disaster-affected people. How did you know it arrived?
2. What happens when a payment stalls or is never claimed? Who finds out, and how long does it take?
3. Who approves a payment? Has one person ever been the only check?
4. Show the case page (UNCLAIMED, 29 days left): what would you do next? What is missing?
5. What would stop you from using this? (Cost, trust, data protection, integration, something else.)
6. Would you use it for your next event? What would you need to see first?

## Consent
Record answers only with consent. Offer to delete on request. Report only real counts and real quotes, with permission.

## After the conversations
- Put each answer in the form above (or have them do it), or paste notes under `docs/VALIDATION_EVIDENCE.md` with the date and the
  person's role (no names unless they agree).
- If fewer than three substantive conversations happened, say so in the submission — the honest status is "unvalidated".
